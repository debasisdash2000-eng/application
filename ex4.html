Apple's iPhone 17 series has opened to an overwhelming response in China, with preorders surpassing last year's iPhone 16 within just a minute of sales going live. According to a report by the South China Morning Post, orders placed on JD.com, one of the country's largest e-commerce platforms, exceeded the entire first-day tally of the iPhone 16 series. The demand was so intense that Apple's website experienced crashes and several buyers reported payment delays of up to five minutes before their orders could be confirmed.

The iPhone 17 Pro Max is leading the charge, cementing its place as the most aspirational model in the lineup. All pickup slots for the device at Apple Stores in Shanghai were taken within 20 minutes, while customers in Guangzhou have already been pushed to delivery dates as far as mid-October.
